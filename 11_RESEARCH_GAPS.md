# Research Gaps

1. **Full verification of 100 technologies:** Only ~10 core technologies received detailed source-backed updates. Remaining TRLs and performance notes are representative.
2. **Materials and critical mineral constraints:** Not explicitly modeled (copper, rare earths, HALEU, lithium, etc.).
3. **Demand-side and economic feedback:** Simulation is supply/growth-oriented; lacks price elasticity, labor markets, or capital allocation dynamics.
4. **Geopolitical and policy scenarios:** Limited treatment of export controls, subsidies, or regional divergence (US/China/EU).
5. **Quantitative techno-economic models** for top innovation projects.
6. **Higher-fidelity dependency data:** Current graph is logically constructed; real citation networks or patent co-occurrence could strengthen it.
7. **Uncertainty quantification beyond triangular distributions.**
