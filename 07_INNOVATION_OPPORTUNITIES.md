# Innovation Opportunities Database
See `innovation_candidates.csv` for the full scored list of 31 candidates.

Candidates were generated by mapping unmet needs and high-centrality bottlenecks from the dependency analysis (power for AI compute, actuators/materials for humanoids, energy supply for scale, cost reduction in biomanufacturing) onto plausible technology fusions.

Scoring weights: Feasibility 20%, Novelty 10%, Scalability 20%, Social Value 15%, Capital Efficiency 15%, Long-term Impact 20%.

Top themes:
1. Energy + AI co-optimization (microreactors, load orchestration, waste-heat recovery)
2. Robotics supply-chain and skill-transfer infrastructure
3. AI-accelerated biomanufacturing and materials discovery
4. Cross-domain enabling platforms (optical interconnects, digital twins, circular systems)
